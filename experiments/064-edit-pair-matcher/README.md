# 064 — promote the ledger's edit-pairing into match-time evidence

> **This is a BRIEF — a hypothesis, including its cautions.**
>
> exp061's fall-through census: 40% of LLM-churned bindings reach the
> ask with a BARE trail — no votes, no match, nothing to carry. Yet the
> 055 measurement ledger recovers their prior names OFFLINE by pairing
> edited statements (same rename-masked head, ≥50% token overlap) and
> walking name-only line pairs. The pipeline never uses that linkage at
> match time.

## Hypothesis

A statement-edit-pair tier — pair hash-flipped statements by masked
head + token overlap, then feed the positional prior names of clean
name-only lines as SUGGESTIONS (never carries) for otherwise-bare
bindings — cuts the bare-trail share of LLM churn materially.

## Cautions pinned before measuring

- exp057: shape-FIFO line pairing is an ARTIFACT; totals derived from
  it are upper bounds. The tier must require unique pairing (exp061's
  skeleton-unique discipline) and unanimity per binding.
- This is a HINT channel like exp061(b) — it inherits the name-
  contention cap measured there; run AFTER exp063 or the yield will be
  eaten by decoration.
- The suggestion source is text alignment, not identity — the role
  gates (callee veto) from single-vote-pin should gate what enters the
  prompt.

## Success criterion (fixed now)

Bare-trail share of llm-terminal churned occurrences (loc-provenance
census) drops materially; paired name-only mass falls below exp063's
result; `novel`/`realLn` byte-exact. Cold trees, ledgers, twice.
